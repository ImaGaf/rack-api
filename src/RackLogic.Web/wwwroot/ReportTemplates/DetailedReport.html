Report Overview

Purpose of the Report

  • To inspect the condition of existing pallet storage racking systems and report on damages that affect the integrity of the rack in accordance with the Australian Standard
  • AS4084:2023 Steel Storage Racking Standard.

Inspection Method

  • A general inspection on ground level, in line‐of‐sight, is conducted where access is possible. In some cases rack cannot be seen where accessibility is inhibited by varying factors, including excess stock on floor, products stored to capacity and height of damage etc. If the inspector has concerns of damage, it may be necessary, where physically possible, to move and/or unload stock to view the area more thoroughly for the inspection to be accurately recorded.
  • The ground level inspection is in accordance with the above standard.

Risk Categories

  • All damage is recorded and reported with a risk indicator to assist the client to understand the level of risk within the work area. This is based on the damage and integrity of the rack and urgency in which repairs and replacements should be approached to remedy the situation and eliminate workplace risk.

Recommendation

  • We highly recommend encouraging staff to report all damages of the racking system as it occurs, especially to upright, beams and braces. The impact can result in unsafe working conditions affecting productivity.

5.5.4 – VERY SERIOUS DAMAGE – "Red" level

When the level of damage exceeds the permissible limits specified in Clauses 5.3.2 to 5.3.4 by more that a factor of 2, it is designated 'red' risk level. The damaged section and any relevant adjacent sections shall be:

  • clearly identified
  • immediately unload
  • isolate

5.5.3 – HAZARDOUS DAMAGE – "Amber" level

When the level of damage exceeds the permissible limits specified in Clauses 5.3.2 to 5.3.4 by a factor between 1 and 2, it is designated "amber" risk level. The damaged section shall be ‐

  • clearly identified
  • unloaded as soon as practicable
  • isolated; and
  • not reloaded until the necessary repairs have been carried out and the equipment assessed as safe for use.

5.5.2 – ACCEPTABLE DAMAGE – "Green" level

When the level of damage does not exceed the permissible limits specified in Clauses 5.3.2 to 5.3.4, the racking shall be deemed serviceable and will not require either unit load reduction or immediate unloading. Damage shall be recorded on the damaged components to indicate that these components have been inspected and the racking is suitable for services until the next inspection when the components will be re‐inspected and re‐assessed (Clause 5.2).
